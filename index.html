How I work

End to end, with AI in the loop.

I like being part of the work from the first interview to the build. AI helps with the slow, repeatable parts. The judgement calls stay with me, and with the people who use the product.

Step 1

Understand

Me

Talk to the people doing the job. Proctra began with a survey of five exam co-ordinators.

AI in the loop

Groups open answers and support tickets into themes. I check every theme against the raw quotes.

Step 2

Frame

Me

Write the rules a screen has to pass before I draw anything.

AI in the loop

Throws edge cases at those rules so the gaps show up early.

Step 3

Design

Me

Build in Figma on real tokens and real data. DigiAssess runs on 35 semantic colours.

AI in the loop

Drafts copy options and fills screens with realistic content.

Step 4

Test

Me

Check contrast, run tasks and watch where people hesitate.

AI in the loop

Writes test scripts and turns session notes into a first summary.

Step 5

Ship

Me

Hand over states, errors and edge cases with engineering in the room.

AI in the loop

Turns specs into working prototypes. The case studies on this site are built that way.

Every AI output gets read by a person before it reaches a user. In Vigil Assist that rule became the product: each line of the AI summary links to its source.

What I bring to a product team

I look for where people get stuck,
and try to make the next step clear.

Rules before screens

Before I draw a screen, I write down what it needs to do. It gives the team something simple to agree on, and the design can be checked against it.

Systems a team can build on
